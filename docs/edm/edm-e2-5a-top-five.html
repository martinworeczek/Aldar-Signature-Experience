●  01 · Signal of the week
 
Oura’s TCX score replaces the survey with the conversation
 
The story
 
Oura built an LLM-based metric that reads every member interaction instead of asking members to rate it afterwards.
  
Why it matters for Aldar
 
Every Aldar line still scores itself on the opinions of the few residents, guests and students who answer a survey. TCX is a working blueprint for scoring the conversation itself, which would give Hospitality and PM & Leasing a read on the whole book rather than the vocal minority.

●  02 · Article · TrendWatch
 
Wellness is being delivered inside transactions customers already make
 
The story
 
Three unrelated operators added wellness to a purchase already happening, rather than building a separate wellness product.
  
Why it matters for Aldar
 
The cheapest wellness proposition Aldar owns is the one attached to a stay, a lease renewal or a mall visit that is already happening. Hospitality has the strongest case, and the after-checkout membership is the part that turns a stay into a relationship.

●  03 · Article · AGBI
 
Dubai starts designing itself away from the car
 
The story
 
A new pedestrian and cycle bridge over Sheikh Zayed Road signals intent about the public realm rather than traffic relief.
  
Why it matters for Aldar
 
New pedestrian infrastructure only earns its cost if arrival on foot is designed as an experience. Every Aldar destination sits at the end of one of these routes, and the last 200 metres are ours to design.
