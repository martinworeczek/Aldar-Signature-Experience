Worth leaving home for.
 
This week’s edition is about the moment a visit stops being a transaction and becomes an experience worth leaving home for. Three stories lead it, and each comes with what it could mean for Aldar.
 
01 · Cosm
 
The film’s own menu, served to your seat
 
Cosm’s cinema domes pair each film with its own menu and bring it to your seat, so dinner becomes part of the show.
 
For Aldar · A mall entertainment visit worth leaving the sofa for: a shared, multi-sensory event, with food timed to the moment.
 
02 · Portland’s science museum
 
A route you choose by mood, not by floor plan
 
Visitors pick their way through the museum by how they want to feel, rather than following a fixed map.
 
For Aldar · School open days and public spaces designed around how people feel, with a calm or energetic path on each visit.
 
03 · Jaguar, London
 
A showroom that opens with lunch, not a pitch
 
Jaguar’s London showroom welcomes visitors to the table before anyone talks about a car.
 
For Aldar · Sales galleries that lead with hospitality and hold the transaction back for a quieter, private space.
