●  01 · Signal of the week
 
Cosm’s Shared Reality cinematic experiences
 
The story
 
Cosm wraps classic films in 12K LED domes, themed venues and scene-timed menus, giving fans a reason to watch familiar titles together rather than at home.
  
Signature Experience opportunity
 
For Retail, it shows how a mall entertainment visit can become a destination worth leaving the sofa for, by building a shared, multi-sensory event around content people already love. For Hospitality, scene-timed themed menus point to F&B that is choreographed with the moment rather than simply served alongside it.

●  02 · Article · Blooloop
 
Climate of Change at OMSI
 
The story
 
Portland’s science museum designed a climate exhibition around how visitors feel, not just what they learn, letting them choose their route by mood and readiness.
  
Signature Experience opportunity
 
For Education, it shows how a school’s learning spaces and open days can be designed around a student’s or parent’s emotional state as much as the content. For Community and Retail, it offers a model for public spaces that let visitors choose a calm or energetic path on each visit.

●  03 · Article · Dezeen
 
Jaguar designs Knightsbridge flagship to be a physical expression of the Type 01 electric car
 
The story
 
Jaguar has turned its London showroom into a destination led by a chef’s restaurant, with interiors that echo its new car and private sales spaces below.
  
Signature Experience opportunity
 
For Development sales galleries and Retail flagships, it shows how the discovery visit can lead with hospitality and place, holding the transaction back for a quieter private space once the visitor is engaged.

●  04 · Article · Retail Design
 
Alaska Airlines Flagship Lounge by Graham Baba Architects
 
The story
 
At Seattle-Tacoma airport, Alaska Airlines moves away from the exclusive business lounge, using a staged entry and four distinct zones to offer every guest a refuge.
  
Signature Experience opportunity
 
For Hospitality and Retail, the lounge shows how an arrival sequence and a choice of distinct moods within one space can make a hotel lobby or a mall visitor lounge feel like a welcoming refuge, rather than a transactional waiting area.

●  05 · Article · HD
 
Lark Hotels writes its own rules
 
The story
 
Lark Hotels runs more than 60 small properties across four brands, each designed around its own building, history and town rather than a single house aesthetic.
  
Signature Experience opportunity
 
For Hospitality, Lark shows how the arrival and first evening can feel like being welcomed by a well-connected local friend rather than a chain. The same local-tastemaker idea could shape how Community teams welcome new residents in their first weeks in a neighbourhood.
